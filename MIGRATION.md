## Compatibility order

Installer and upgrade discovery use this order:

1. `runovelhq/tge`
2. `AlirezaSayyari/TGE`
3. `AlirezaSayyari/V2rayTGE`

GitHub repository-transfer redirects are helpful, but canonical-first discovery does not depend solely on redirects. The compatibility references are temporary and will be removed only through a future announced compatibility change.
